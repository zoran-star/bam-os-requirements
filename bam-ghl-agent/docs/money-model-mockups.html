Money model mockups

Five surfaces for the tax template, sign-up fee, and coupon applicability builds. Infrastructure only: nothing is applied to any academy until you say so. Values shown use BAM San Jose's real shape ($40 fee, no tax) and BAM GTA's (13% HST, 2SIBLING).

Mockup 4 · Build C

Coupon editor: what does this code apply to?

Academy owners create their own discount codes, and every sellable price and every fee is its own checkbox. Today a code silently hits every line on the invoice; after this, the owner chooses. GTA's 2SIBLING migrates with all current prices checked, which is exactly its behavior today.

Code: 2SIBLING · 50% off · every payment

Applies to

Plan: Steady

Every 4 weeks $226.00
Sign-up fee $45.20 one time
Part of Steady. Charged only on the every-4-weeks option; commitments waive it.

Plan: Summer Unlimited

Every 4 weeks $315.27
3 months $850.89
No sign-up fee on this plan, so there is nothing extra to check.
The fee is nested under its plan because that is how it is stored: it is a price row of the SAME plan (Steady|signup_fee), not a separate product floating on its own. A fee checkbox only appears under plans that have one. Unchecked lines are never discounted, even on a shared invoice.

Sign-up fee shown as a hypothetical here: GTA has no fee configured today. The nesting is the point.
