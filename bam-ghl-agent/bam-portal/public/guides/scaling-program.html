The Scaling Program

How a client gets on the program, how the 7 calls work, where the notes live, and how the money runs each month.

Staff only - nothing in this system is visible to clients

1Put a client on the program

  1. Open Commissions in the staff portal sidebar (admin only for edits).
  2. Find the academy - unconfigured ones say "not configured".
  3. Click the row to expand the terms form.
  4. Pick the model: Flat Retainer (fixed monthly fee) or Growth % (base retainer + share of growth).
  5. For Growth %: enter base retainer, growth share %, revenue baseline, renewal date, revenue source, and confirm the Scaling Manager.
  6. Hit Save terms. That save IS the switch - the client is now on the program.
The baseline locks for 9 months the moment you save it. Changing it inside the window forces an explicit override and re-locks for another 9. Only re-enter it on agreement renewal.

2Work the 7 calls

Once terms are saved, the call sequence appears on the client's Onboarding tab (staff portal > Clients > pick the client). Calls run in strict order - Call 3 stays locked until Call 2 is marked complete. Only the SM ticks them.

Every call opens with the same calibration question: "What do you currently have in place for this?" Type the answers into the call's fields during the call - that typing IS the documentation.

3The Profile tab

Each client has a Profile tab next to Onboarding. It shows everything from the 7 calls on one page, grouped by topic, so nobody digs through completed checklist items to answer "what's this client's offer structure".

  1. It is the same record as the call fields - not a copy. Edit in either place, both update.
  2. Notes appear here the moment they are typed, even before the call is ticked done.
  3. Topics with a real home elsewhere link out: Offers to the Business Blueprint snapshot, funnels and ads to Systems / Marketing.
Discipline note: the Profile page is only as good as what gets typed on the call. Empty fields on the call = empty profile.

4The money each month

Each program client runs on their own cycle, anchored to their renewal date (not the 1st). At cycle close the portal pulls the month's gross revenue from the academy's connected Stripe and computes:

growth = max(0, gross revenue - baseline) BAM fee = growth share % x growth BAM invoice = base retainer + BAM fee (flat model: just the flat amount) SM payout = $250 + 25% x BAM fee (only in months with growth)

5Quick answers

What does the client see?
Nothing. No checklist items, no profile page, no commission info. The only client-visible artifact is the normal Stripe invoice when it is sent.
Who can do what?
Admin: set terms, see every client. Scaling Manager: see own clients and their cycles, edit call notes, cannot touch payment terms. Everyone else: no Commissions tab at all.
How do I take a client OFF the program?
Clear their payment model in the Commissions tab. Call steps and profile content disappear from staff views; the notes stay in the database and come back if re-enabled.
Refunds?
The pull counts raw gross charges. Refunds and chargebacks are NOT netted out - flag disputes to Mike before the cycle closes if they are material.
Which clients can go on the program?
V2 and V1.5 academies. Legacy V1 accounts are excluded by rule.
