Hi Maya, now that Jordan Alvarez is part of the By Any Means family, here is how to get the absolute most out of every single session.
1
Talk to coach.
Every player can reach our coaches anytime, about anything - basketball, school, or life. Before practice, tell coach what you want to work on and we will build the session around it.
2
Learn your weaknesses.
Come ready to share what you want to improve. The more honest you are about the weak spots, the faster the growth.
3
Do the extra work.
Treat training like a class - there is homework too. Stay consistent with the online programs and notebook work, even 20 minutes a day moves the needle.
