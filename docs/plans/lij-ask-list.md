# What we need from you, Lij

Short version: one thing is holding everything up, and the rest is quick.

Anything not on this list, we already have. Your reviews, your gym addresses, your domain and your business number are all in and done.

## 1. Connect Stripe. This is the one that matters.

Everything downstream is waiting on it: taking payments, your prices going live, the sign-up fee, the enrollment page, and even your class times generating in the system.

We send you a link, you click through Stripe's setup with your business details and bank account. Usually 10 to 15 minutes.

**If you do nothing else on this list, do this one.**

---

## 2. Your coaches

We have 4 people on your team and no details for any of them, so right now the system talks about "the coaches" in general terms instead of naming who your athletes actually train with.

For each coach, including you:

- Title (Head Coach, Skills Coach, whatever you use)
- A few sentences on their background

Your own is the most valuable. It is what turns "our coaches" into real credentials in front of a parent.

## 3. Google reviews: add us as a manager

You have 22 reviews at 5.0, and we have already pulled 5 of them in.

To keep them current we need **manager access on your Google Business listing**, not just the link. The public page cuts long reviews off partway and there is no way around that from the outside.

In Google Business Profile: Settings, then People and access, then add us as a Manager.

## 8. Your own Twilio account

This is for texting and calling long term. Set up the account under your business and we will handle the wiring.

Your EIN goes into your own Twilio profile when you get there, so you do not need to send it to us.

**One thing to know now so it is not a surprise later.** On the day we switch your number across, outbound texting has a gap of a few hours, up to about 2 days worst case. Calls keep working the whole time and incoming texts are only down for minutes.

That switch happens weeks **after** you go live, not before. Nothing about it delays your launch.
