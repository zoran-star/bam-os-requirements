The design in one line

Reignition is its own stage on the board. Staff hand-picks who goes in, writes the campaign's messages fresh, and a paced drip plays them out - a reply hands the person to the sales system, silence hands them to nurture.

The stage is defined by ROLE, not by name, so it bolts onto the free-trial system, the discovery-call system, and every sales system we ever build, unchanged.

The free-trial system and the discovery-call system both have a front stage and a nurture stage, so this bolts onto either today, and onto any future system that has them, with zero changes. The only way in is a campaign roster. No form feeds it, no automation feeds it, nothing enters by accident.

A campaign's life

Draft costs nothing and sends nothing. The dry run is the gate.

1 · draft
Build the roster
Staff picks people by hand. Tags and filters narrow the list; nobody is added by a rule. Repeat visitors from old campaigns are allowed.
2 · draft
Write the messages
Fresh for this campaign: 1-3 steps, texts or emails or both, with their waits. Rendered exactly as they will send.
3 · the gate
Dry run
The exact roster, the count, and everyone the rails removed, with reasons. Nothing has sent yet. Staff approves here.
4 · running
The drip
15 people a day enter the stage and get step 1. Each person's later steps follow the campaign's own waits, inside their local hours.
5 · done
Or halted
Replies leave as they happen. Halt at any moment: whoever has not been reached is simply never reached.
The pacing trick: we pace the door, not the messages

The drip admits up to the daily number of people INTO the stage each day; once someone is in, their messages just follow the campaign's own waits. Pacing never touches the send machinery, so every guard that already protects sends - quiet hours, time zones, empty-message skips, the works - applies untouched. 60 people at 15 a day means the campaign takes four days to fully launch.

The rails, none of them optional

Some people can never be added, no matter who tries

Current members. Anyone already live in a pipeline stage. Anyone marked do-not-disturb or who ever unsubscribed or complained. Anyone with no working phone or email for the campaign's channels. The roster builder refuses them with the reason shown, and there is no override.

And one honest limit, checked against the live data: the system only records who OPTED OUT. "This person consented" does not exist as data anywhere, so no automatic check can prove it - a check that waved through everyone without an opt-out would protect nobody. So every campaign carries a consent basis, written by a person: where these leads came from and why we may message them. It is recorded on the campaign, and the dry run shows it beside the roster.

Quarantined imports stay quiet until a campaign names them

Leads seeded from an academy's old system land quarantined. A reignition roster is the ONLY path by which any of them is ever messaged. Importing someone's leads can never, by itself, cause a single message.

A reply ends the campaign for that person, instantly

Their remaining steps are cancelled, they move to the front stage, and the booking agent takes over mid-conversation. Nobody gets campaign step 3 after they already answered step 1.

They become ordinary leads the moment it works

After the campaign there is nothing special about them. The agent works them, ghosted catches the quiet ones, nurture catches the rest. No parallel track exists to maintain.
