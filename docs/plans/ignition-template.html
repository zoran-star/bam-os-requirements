Ignition:
waking up leads they already have.

Every academy arrives with a pile of old leads nobody followed up. Ignition is the warm, safe way to put those people back into the sales system, one campaign at a time, without blasting anyone. Back to the five skills →

The idea in one line

An ignition is just another front door into the pipeline, exactly like the free-trial form is, except the people come from a tag instead of a form.

That one decision is what makes it a template. The pipeline already understands "here is a door, here is who comes through it, here is what we say when they do." Ignition is a third kind of door, so every sales system we ever build gets it for free.

Why it fits without bending anything

The sales system already has two kinds of door. We are adding a third, not building a parallel system.

Because a door is declared against a stage role rather than a named stage, the same ignition works on any sales system that has that role. Our second preset, the discovery-call system, already does. It inherits ignition the day this ships, with no extra work.

What you fill in to make one

Six things. Everything else is the template.

1
Who
The tag that defines the audience, plus how cold is too cold. Anyone carrying the tag whose last contact was longer ago than your floor.
tag: summer-2025-enquiry  ·  last heard from: more than 60 days ago
2
Who never, no matter what
Exclusions that are not optional and cannot be switched off: current members, anyone already live in a stage, anyone who unsubscribed or complained, anyone with no consent on file, and anyone with no working phone or email.
always excluded · members · already active · unsubscribed · complained · no consent
3
Which door they come through
Warm people go to the front of the pipeline and get worked by the booking agent. Cold people go to the long game instead. You pick per campaign.
into: Booking  (or Nurture, for the colder list)
4
What we open with
The one genuinely written part. A new lead gets "thanks for your enquiry"; someone from eight months ago needs a message that admits time has passed and gives them a reason to care now. It goes through the same review and the same owner approval as every other message.
the reignition opener · 1 to 3 messages · written per campaign
5
How fast
A drip, never a blast. So many per day, inside their own hours and their own time zone. Sixty-five texts at once is how a number gets flagged by the carriers.
pace: 15 a day · their local hours only
6
How it stops
A reply pulls that person straight out of the campaign and into the normal pipeline, immediately. And you can halt the whole thing mid-flight: anyone not yet reached is simply never reached.
reply → out of the campaign, into Booking  ·  halt → nobody else is sent to

The rails, because this is the one part that can go badly wrong

Nothing sends until you say who

Seeded leads land quarantined. An ignition campaign is the only way any of them ever gets a message, and every campaign names its audience out loud and is approved before it runs. There is no path where importing someone's leads causes messages to go out.

A dry run first, every time

Before anything sends, you see the exact count, a sample of who is in, and everyone the exclusions removed and why. If that list looks wrong, nothing has happened yet.

One person, one ignition, ever

Somebody who has already been reignited is not eligible again, so overlapping campaigns cannot double-text the same family. It is remembered per person, not per campaign.

They are ordinary leads the moment it works

After the opener, nothing about them is special. The booking agent works them, ghosted catches the quiet ones, nurture catches the rest. No parallel track to maintain.

How this plugs into bringing their leads across

You described three kinds of lead when we seed an academy. Ignition is what the third kind needs, and it is why designing it first was right.

Direct
recent · already moving

Lands exactly where they were, on the board, mid-conversation. No message at all. They should not notice anything happened.

Altered
their stage does not map to ours

Our best guess at the right stage, shown to staff as an editable list before anything is written. No message, just placed correctly.

Reignited
cold · nobody followed up

Held in quarantine until an ignition campaign wakes them, warmly and paced. This is the only group that gets messaged.

Their pipeline will not look like ours, and that is the normal case

Academies name their stages whatever they like and let leads go stale in them. So the migration never assumes a mapping. It reads their board, makes its best guess at which of our stages each lead belongs in, and hands staff an editable list to work through before a single lead moves. The guess is a starting point, not a decision.

What I need from you

Workshop this, then tell me it is right. Three things I have decided that you may want differently:

1. The opener is written per campaign, not per academy. A summer special and a we-moved-gyms need different words, and no template can guess them.

2. Warm goes to Booking so the AI agent works them. The alternative is dropping them in Nurture and letting the emails do it, which is slower but gentler.

3. One ignition per person, ever. Strict, and it means a great campaign next year cannot re-reach someone reignited this year.
