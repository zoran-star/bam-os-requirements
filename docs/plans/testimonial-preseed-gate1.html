Why gathering is not inside skill 3

You isolated skill 3 because more sales systems are coming, each with its own build. An academy's testimonials are not owned by a sales system: the same quotes serve the free trial, the next system, and member emails. Putting gathering inside skill 3 means the second sales system either re-asks for them or forks. The store is shared machinery; only the skill forks.

The ordering reason, which is the harder one

Your rule is that an empty store drops the email, and that is decided when the system is seeded, not when the email sends. Skill 3 is the seeder. So the quotes have to already be in place before skill 3 runs, or the step gets dropped and somebody has to re-seed later to bring it back.
