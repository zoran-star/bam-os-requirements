From Lij's answers to Stripe

The whole trip, in five steps. His members never notice anything happened - that is the point.

1
Lij fills in the workbook

His plans, prices, prepay deals, tax answer, discount code. Nothing he types touches Stripe. It is a form, not a control panel.

"2x/week is $250 every 4 weeks, $599 for 3 months, joining fee waived on prepay, no sales tax."
2
Our team checks every answer

In Claude, card by card. Every change he made is shown side by side with what we had. Nothing moves forward without a human yes on each card.

What actually lives inside his Stripe

Stripe is like a shop's back office. Five kinds of paper matter here:

The menu item "Academy 2x/week" as a thing he sells [a Product]. He has 27 of these, some messy: his Unlimited prices sit under one named "Black Friday Offer".
The price tag One menu item can carry several tags: $250 every 4 weeks, $599 per 12 weeks, $1,150 per 24 weeks [Prices with a recurring interval]. He has 119 tags, only 14 in use. The $40 joining fee is a tag with no repeat [a one-time Price].
The parent's file card Name, card on file, history [a Customer]. He has 147.
The standing order "This customer pays this price tag every cycle" [a Subscription]. His 20 members are 20 of these, and each bill it produces is an [Invoice]; the joining fee rides the first invoice as an extra line [an invoice item].
The money-off stamp His "club" coupon, $100 off [a Coupon, which can be limited to certain menu items via applies_to]. The word a parent could type at checkout to use it is separate [a Promotion Code], and he has none, which is why nobody has ever used club.
3
The system looks inside his Stripe first

For every price on the approved list it asks one question: does this already exist in his account?

Already there link it A price tag counts as "the same" only if the amount AND the billing rhythm both match [unit_amount + recurring interval]. Then we point at it and create nothing; his members keep paying on the exact same tag they always have.
San Jose: all 12 plan prices were found. $250 every 4 weeks, $599 per 12 weeks... all already his.
Not there create it We write a new price tag into HIS account, under his menu item, only after approval [a new Price on his connected account].
San Jose: only the four $40 joining fees need creating, one no-repeat tag per plan.
4
The stamp

Approved answers are written into the portal, and each portal price now holds its Stripe twin's serial number [the price id, stored as stripe_price_id]. From this moment the portal and his Stripe say the same thing, provably: anyone can follow the serial number and check.

5
Everything downstream reads the stamp

The sales agent quotes these prices. Checkout charges them. Receipts print them. Nobody retypes a number anywhere, so nothing can drift.

What never happens: we never edit or delete a price his members are paying on, we never touch his account without a per-card approval, and until the final go the whole thing runs in rehearsal mode where Stripe only gets read, never written.
