Social proof is the one hole, and it is the same hole as the testimonials email

Nothing fills it in, so the agents have no reviews or parent quotes to draw on. It is deliberately switched off rather than guessing, and it closes when the Google Reviews work lands. Worth knowing that the same missing thing costs us an email and an agent's best argument.

What this adds up to

Of the whole sales system, two emails need a person, and the skill drafts both of them from words the owner already gave us. Everything else copies or fills itself in. The two real holes are social proof and testimonials, and they are the same hole.
