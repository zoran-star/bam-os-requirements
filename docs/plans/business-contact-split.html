Business contact, separate from owner contact

Four things to decide. Everything below is verified against the live database and the real code today, and the two phone numbers are marked as candidates rather than facts because that is what they are.

The problem, in one line

Every BAM GTA email published to a parent carries zoran@byanymeansbball.com as its contact and its unsubscribe address.

There is no field for an academy's business email, so the system uses the owner's personal one. Today GTA is saved only by a hardcoded exception in the code; delete that exception and your inbox becomes the public contact for every GTA email.

What changes

Two new fields on the academy, beside the owner's, not replacing them. The owner's email and phone keep meaning exactly what they mean now.

Riding along: the Google rating

Another room owns these three fields. They are listed here because the card gets edited once, by one person, rather than twice. I am placing them, not designing them.

FieldWhat it holds
Google rating1.0 to 5.0
Review countHow many reviews that rating is built on
Checked onThe date somebody actually looked. Stamped automatically, never typed.
It is shown as a dated reading, never as "your rating"

On screen it reads "Google showed 4.9 from 67 reviews on 29 Jul", not "Google rating: 4.9".

The reason is subtle and worth a sentence. These numbers get pulled from your signed-in Google view rather than typed, which sounds more trustworthy. It is actually the opposite risk: a number we genuinely fetched looks current, when it may be weeks stale - the next review lands and nothing tells us. A typed number invites doubt; a fetched one does not. So the date is not decoration, it is the part that keeps it honest.

The rating and the count are one thing to fill in, not two. Setting one without the other is rejected by the database on purpose.

Decide these four

Each has a recommendation. Say yes to all four and this is ready to build.

Decision 2

When an academy has no business email, refuse or fall back?

If the field is empty, the system either publishes nothing, or quietly reaches for the owner's personal address. Falling back would reinstate the exact bug we are removing, and make it harder to spot, because the field would look configured.

Recommendation: hold it AND say so. Not just refuse - refusing quietly is its own failure mode, because emails stop going out and nobody finds out until someone notices the silence.

You already have this exact behaviour running in production: when an academy's sending domain is not set up, the system holds the emails and texts the owner to say why, and will not text them again about the same thing. Held emails then go out on their own once setup finishes. Same rule here, same reason, and it is already proven rather than proposed.
Decision 3

Must the business email be proven connected, not just typed?

An address saved in a box is not an address that works. If it is not verified for sending and not connected for receiving, you get a from-address that looks configured, bounces, and sends every parent reply into a void nobody checks.

Recommendation: yes, prove it. This is the third time today the same failure shape has shown up: something that reports readiness it does not actually have. The setup step should refuse to call itself done until both directions are proven.
Decision 4

What happens to GTA's hardcoded exception?

GTA's business email lives in a hardcoded block in the code. Once the new field exists, keeping both means two sources of truth for the same fact, with the code silently winning. That is the thing we have spent the week removing.

Recommendation: delete it in this build, once GTA's row is filled and the footer is verified reading the new field. Not left as a backup. A backup that overrides the real value is not a backup.

Two facts I checked rather than assumed

San Jose's owner alerts DO work

An earlier finding said San Jose had no phone numbers at all, so every owner guardrail was silently dead. That is now out of date. Elijah is on the row with a phone, so alerts reach him. Verified in the live database today.

Only the owner has a phone, at both academies

GTA has 5 team members and San Jose has 4. At both, only the owner has a phone number saved. Anything meant to reach a wider team by text reaches one person. Not part of this build, but worth knowing.

What this unblocks

The last GTA-specific hardcode can finally be removed

The blocker was never technical. Removing GTA's hardcoded block would have put your personal inbox in the footer and the unsubscribe link of every GTA email, so it could not be removed. With a business email field, that stops being a decision and becomes ordinary work.
