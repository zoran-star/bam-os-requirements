Trial confirmations: right time for every academy

Lij onboarding, queue item 4 · plan for approval
When a parent books a free trial, the confirmation text and email currently show the session time in Toronto time, no matter where the academy is. A San Jose parent booking a 5pm trial gets a message saying 8pm. This fix makes every confirmation show the time in that academy's own timezone, and the "good to go for today?" morning check-in will also fire at 9am local instead of 6am California time.

1What the San Jose parent receives

SMS · today
BEFORE - wrong
Your free trial is booked! Here are some important details: Date & Time: Fri, Jul 31 at 8:00 PM Location: 1234 Blossom Hill Rd, San Jose, CA Please bring a basketball if you have one. Prepare to be challenged, so come ready to work! ...
Trial is at 5:00 PM in San Jose. Text says 8:00 PM (Toronto time). Parent shows up 3 hours late or calls confused.
SMS · after the fix
AFTER - correct
Your free trial is booked! Here are some important details: Date & Time: Fri, Jul 31 at 5:00 PM Location: 1234 Blossom Hill Rd, San Jose, CA Please bring a basketball if you have one. Prepare to be challenged, so come ready to work! ...
Same booking, rendered in the academy's timezone. Toronto academies keep seeing exactly what they see today.
Your free trial is booked! 🏀From: BAM San Jose
Date & Time: Fri, Jul 31 at 8:00 PM Location: 1234 Blossom Hill Rd, San Jose, CA Apple / Google calendar links...
Your free trial is booked! 🏀From: BAM San Jose
Date & Time: Fri, Jul 31 at 5:00 PM Location: 1234 Blossom Hill Rd, San Jose, CA Apple / Google calendar links...
Bonus fix, same change: the same-day "are we good to go?" check-in is timed by the same hardcoded clock. Today it would hit a San Jose phone at 6:00 AM. After the fix it fires at 9:00 AM local. Calendar links (Apple / Google) are already stored in universal time and stay correct on their own; they do not change.

2How it decides the timezone

Academy row: is time_zone set?
(San Jose gets "America/Los_Angeles" saved during onboarding - a per-academy fact, no code fork)
↓ yes
↓ no, or the value is invalid
USE IT
All confirmation times and the 9am check-in run on the academy's own clock. Any real timezone works (US, Canada, overseas).
FALL BACK TO TORONTO + SEND + TELL THE OWNER
The receipt still goes out, in Toronto time. Then the academy owner gets a one-time heads-up text (max once per day, never silent):
Heads up: your academy's timezone is not set, so booking texts show Toronto time. Fix it in Settings > Time zone in your portal.
Sent over the existing owner-SMS notifier (same channel as "new booking" and "ticket completed" texts). One tap to fix, then it never fires again.
Why fall back instead of holding the message: a booking receipt is transactional and must always send, and every academy with a blank timezone today actually IS Toronto, so falling back is always correct right now. (Making timezone a required onboarding fact is queue item 10, not this build.)

Why the owner gets the heads-up (not BAM staff): the timezone field lives in the owner's own portal Settings (they set it themselves), so the owner is the one person who can fix it in one tap.

Exact behaviour

  • {{appointment.start_time}}, only_start_time, only_start_date render in resolveClientTz(client).
  • morning_of due-check (send_hour, default 9) and day_before day-diff evaluate against the academy's local calendar, same tz.
  • NULL / empty / invalid clients.time_zone: send anyway in America/Toronto (matches quietTz behaviour everywhere else), then text the owner the timezone-missing heads-up, max once per 24h per academy.
  • Guardrail recipient: the academy OWNER (they own the Settings field that fixes it); channel is the existing notifyOwners owner-SMS, no new channel invented.
  • Rollout step (ops, not code): before merge, backfill time_zone for existing academies (GTA = "America/Toronto", San Jose = "America/Los_Angeles") so nobody gets a pointless nag on day one.
  • Shared code, zero forks: no academy names, no per-academy branches. San Jose is fixed purely by setting its clients.time_zone = "America/Los_Angeles" row value (one-line ops step at rollout, belongs to the onboarding checklist).

Edge cases

  • DST: IANA zone via Intl handles it automatically; verify a date on each side of a DST switch (check #4).
  • NULL tz: falls back to Toronto and still sends (check #5).
  • Invalid tz string (typo like "America/San_Jose"): validator falls back to Toronto instead of the fmt* catch silently deleting the Date & Time line.
  • Non-US zone: any valid IANA name (e.g. "Europe/London") just works; nothing assumes North America.
  • Midnight straddle: a 9pm PT booking is "tomorrow" in Toronto; day_before / morning_of must use the academy-local date (they do once tz is threaded through dayDiffInTz).
  • Alert race: two cron runs hitting the same academy in the same minute - the 24h check reads tz_alert_at and the stamp is PATCHed before notifyOwners fires, so a double-text needs a sub-second overlap (acceptable; the notifier itself is best-effort).
  • Alert failure: notifyOwners is non-throwing; a failed heads-up never blocks or delays the parent's receipt.

  5. Fail-safe: client rows with time_zone NULL and time_zone = "not/a_zone" both render Toronto times, no crash, Date & Time line present.
  6. Calendar links untouched: the Google Calendar dates= UTC string and the /api/ical URL are identical before vs after the change for the same booking.
