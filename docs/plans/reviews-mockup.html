Two things a typed-in quote can never do
Wear a rating or a Google badge
No stars, no "Google review" line, no review date. Only a synced review earns those.
Move the average
The aggregate counts synced Google reviews only. Typing in ten quotes cannot make a 4.9 into a 5.0.
Where this sits on your control dial
Tier 1The hierarchy itself
Locked, identical for every academy, auto-propagates. No academy reorders it, and nobody gets a custom rule.
Tier 3The quotes themselves
The academy's own facts: its reviews, its typed quotes, which ones it pins. Never propagates to anyone else.

This is the same shape as the rest of the dial: the rule is master and shared, the content is the academy's own. It applies everywhere a quote can appear, which is the website cards, the testimonial emails, and the agent.

Three things to decide

  1. One table or two. A builder is right now creating a testimonials table for the email work, and my plan called for a separate google_reviews table. I say one table, with Google reviews syncing into it, or we end up with two versions of the same quote. I have already sent the builder the four columns it is missing (star rating above all, since every curation rule depends on it).
  2. Do typed-in quotes survive? The new table lets an academy type a quote in by hand, which is a testimonial with no Google review behind it, the thing your rule was aimed at. My recommendation: allow them, but they never get review framing. No star rating, no "Google review" badge, no counting toward the average. Only real synced reviews earn stars.
  3. The cheap early win. That same migration adds a per-academy Google review link field. Filling it in fixes the San Jose and Miami agents sending GTA's link, with no Google API approval needed, in days rather than weeks. You said leave it until we connect, when connecting meant the full build. This is a much smaller kind of connected. Want it now?

The full build still needs Google to approve our Cloud project for the Business Profile API, which is days to weeks and has not been filed yet. That application is the long pole on everything in screens 2 through 6.
