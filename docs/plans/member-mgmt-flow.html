Top: where it sits in onboarding an academy. Middle: the pipeline for San Jose. Bottom: the quality loop protecting the send.

→
Core sitetheir website, free trial page

Member managementTHIS BUILD: existing members + pricing move into the portalhere

Why it exists: an academy like San Jose already has paying members in its own Stripe. Before the portal can bill, quote, or route anyone, what they pay and who they are has to come across correctly, confirmed by the owner, with nobody double-charged. San Jose is the proving run; the skills get written from what actually happens, so academy #4 is a repeat, not a rebuild.

4 · The quality loop (running right now)

Rehearsalan agent plays Lij on the real page, real database, Stripe in dry run
→
Plannerturns findings into an executable plan
→
Builderbuilds it, commits per step

Tester fails it → back to the planner. Tester passes → rehearsal runs again from the top. The link goes to Lij only when a full rehearsal comes back clean. So far: round 1 caught 9 bugs, round 2 confirmed all fixes and caught 1 blocker, round 3 is next.
