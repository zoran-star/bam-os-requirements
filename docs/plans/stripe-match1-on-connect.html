Contact match, running by itself

The client clicks Connect Stripe once. This is what should happen next, and the one thing that must not.

The hazard I found before building it

Matching cannot be allowed to CREATE people automatically. Today the staff tool does three things: link the obvious ones, ask a human about the unclear ones, and invent a new person when it has never seen them. That third one is safe when a human runs it after the academy's contacts are loaded. It is not safe on connect, because Stripe is usually connected before the contacts arrive, and every invented person becomes a duplicate of the real one that lands later.

The fix is a one-word change to what runs automatically: it LINKS, it never CREATES. Anyone it cannot confidently match goes on a short staff list in the portal, where "this is a new person, add them" is one click. Nothing is lost, nothing is invented behind our backs, and the client still never touches it.

Your call: where does this live

Two new skills
A "Stripe setup" skill and a "price match" skill, sitting next to the branding deck, the site build and the sales system.
  • Shows up on the onboarding checklist like everything else
  • Obvious to a new staff member that it exists
  • Two more things on a list that is already long
Two phases inside member management
Both live inside the member management one, because they only ever exist to make members work.
  • Keeps everything about paying members in one place
  • Fewer moving parts on the checklist
  • Easier to miss, because it is buried one level down

Either way the automatic half is identical. This only changes where the human-facing half is written down.
