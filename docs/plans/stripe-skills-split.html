Splitting anywhere else means one skill sitting and waiting on something the other half never needed. Contact match has no reason to wait for pricing, and price match cannot run without it.

What still needs a person

Two parents sharing an email. A match on phone only. A customer already linked to somebody else. These are judgement calls and no rule fixes them. They come back as a short review list.

But that is staff work, in the portal. The client never sees it, and it never blocks them.

The result: the client clicks connect, whenever suits them, and that is the last thing they touch. Nothing waits on a staff member remembering a button.
