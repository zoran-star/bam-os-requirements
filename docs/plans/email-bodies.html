Two things only you can decide

Everything else on this page I can do without asking.

1. What do the schedule groups get called?

Your email says "Younger" and "Older". Your actual sessions are named "Group 1 (Elementary)" and "Group 2 (High School)". Generating the schedule from real sessions means picking which wording a parent reads.

Keep "Younger" and "Older"
Nothing your parents read changes. Every academy gets the same two labels, which may not fit an academy that splits by anything other than age.
Use your real session names
"Group 1 (Elementary) 7-8pm". More accurate, works for any academy however they split their groups, but it is a visible change to your email.
2. The training video in email two

The "Attention to Detail" video is your own YouTube content. Right now it is hardcoded, so every academy would send yours.

Give every academy a video field
They paste their own link. No link means the email drops the video section and still reads fine.
Drop the video from the shared version
It stays in yours as your own content and never travels. Simpler, but a new academy's second email is thinner.
