The Product

Beyond all-in-one

All-in-one is the floor. Three things separate FullControl from the pack:

Why now: software that acts on its own became possible only in the last two years. Nobody native to this vertical has claimed it, and the incumbents would need a rebuild, not a feature.

1

Talk to your business

Voice or text as the interface to your entire operation. “Cancel tomorrow’s session, give credits, and boost my ad spend while you’re at it.” → Done. The system executes across scheduling, billing, comms, and ads in a single sentence.

2

It runs while you sleep

Overnight agents do the operational work most operators do reactively. Conversational AI follows up with warm leads. Ad spend rebalances based on performance. Parent comms queue for approval. At-risk players are flagged with notes. You wake up to drafts, not a blank inbox.

In beta today: Hawkeye, our sales agent, runs real parent conversations for our academies right now - drafting every message overnight while the owner approves, edits, or redirects from a card deck each morning. Nothing sends without a human tap. Named agents for marketing and member management follow the same playbook next.

3

The more it learns, the more control you have

FullControl personalizes to your specific program over time. Your roster, your players, your communication style, your cadence. The longer you use it, the more leverage you get from it. This is already running in beta: every time an owner edits an AI draft, the system asks why and stores the correction as a lesson, so the agents write more like that academy every week.
